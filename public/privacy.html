🔍 Chess Detectives — Privacy

Last updated: 5 November 2026 · applies to the app (iPhone, iPad) and the website.

The short version

Chess Detectives has no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We do not collect, sell or share your personal data. Your progress stays on your device.

What the app uses

  • Your chess.com username. You type it in; the app downloads your public games from chess.com's public API to find your mistakes. The username and games are stored only on your device.
  • Your progress (XP, puzzles, notes, saved opening lines) is stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes it.
  • Stockfish, the chess engine, and Professor Pin's voice run on your device. Nothing you play is sent anywhere.

Other services you may choose to open

  • chess.com — to load public games, and when you tap "Open game". See chess.com's privacy policy.
  • YouTube — the Chess Detectives video lessons open in YouTube. See Google's privacy policy.
  • Natural voice (optional) — if you pick it in voice settings, a free voice model is downloaded once from a public file server. No personal data is sent.
  • Sign in with Google (website only, optional) — if the website owner turned it on and you sign in, your progress is saved in your own Google Firebase account area so it can sync between devices. You can sign out at any time.

Children

Chess Detectives is made to be family-friendly. It does not ask children for personal information and does not show ads. A chess.com username is the only thing typed in.
