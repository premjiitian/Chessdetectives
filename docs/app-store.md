**Description**:
Become a chess detective! Chess Detectives looks at your chess.com games and finds the moments that cost you the most, then turns them into puzzles made just for you.

• Your mistakes, your puzzles — over 7 days, a month, a year or more
• Professor Pin explains why the best move works, in plain words
• Tap ▶ Listen for a short spoken summary: what went right or wrong, your opponent's best answer and the plan for both sides
• Stockfish 19, the world's strongest engine, runs right on your iPhone or iPad — and plays back like a real opponent
• Your opening repertoire for White and Black, in your own style, with stronger moves where you need them. Add your own lines and notes
• 20 video chapters from the Chess Detectives course, each with practice positions from your games
• XP, ranks, streaks and badges that grow with every puzzle
• Spar against friendly levels made for kids, all the way up to full strength
• Works offline after your games are loaded. No ads, no tracking — your progress stays on your device

Just type your chess.com username to start. Chess Detectives is not affiliated with chess.com.
